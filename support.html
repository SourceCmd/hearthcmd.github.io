Support

Hearth Cmd runs your household's agents on your keep: the Keep Cmd software on a computer you own. Most problems come down to whether the keep is running and reachable. This page covers the common ones.

Troubleshooting

The app says my keep is offline

  • Check that the keep is running on its computer:
keepcmd status
  • If it isn't, start it:
keepcmd start
  • Check that the computer is on, awake and connected to the internet. While the keep is offline, the app may show your household read-only, from the last data it saw.
  • The keep's log is at ~/.keepcmd/daemon.log on that computer. It's designed to hold identifiers and error codes, not your content.

An agent won't start

  • Make sure the keep is running (keepcmd status).
  • Make sure the AI software the agent uses (for example, Claude Code) is installed and signed in on the keep's computer. Try running it directly in a terminal.
  • Check the keep's log for errors.

An agent keeps asking for the same thing

  • Tap Always allow instead of Allow to make a rule, so matching requests go ahead without asking. Network access for agents is closed by default and builds up this way.

I'm not getting notifications

  • On your iPhone, open Settings → Hearth Cmd → Notifications and check that notifications are allowed.
  • If you use a Focus mode, allow Hearth Cmd in it.

I got a new phone

  • Sign in on the new phone with the same email address, and link it from a device you already have when the app asks.

Deleting your account

Open the Account tab and tap Delete account. Your keep and its data stay on your computer; remove them there if you want them gone. See the Privacy Policy for what's deleted.
