Support

Hearth Cmd runs your household's agents on your Keep: the Keep Cmd software on a computer you own. Most problems come down to whether the Keep is running and reachable. This page covers the common ones. Using Hearth Cmd Classic? See below.

Getting started

  • Connecting to your Keep. On your Keep's computer, run keepcmd hub pair --app hearth to show a pairing code. With your phone on the same Wi-Fi, choose Use on your home network only in Hearth Cmd and scan it, or paste its link. No account is needed.
  • Signing in. To use Hearth Cmd away from home, join a household or invite people, sign in with your email address or phone number, at the start or later in Account → Away from Home. If Loop Cmd is signed in on the same phone, Hearth Cmd offers to continue as that account with one tap.
  • Joining a household. If someone invited you, sign in with the email address or phone number they used, and accept the invitation. Then wait for the person who invited you to let you in: their phone shows a code, so check it matches the one on yours.

Common questions

The app says my Keep is offline

  • Check that the Keep is running on its computer:
keepcmd status
  • If it isn't, start it:
keepcmd start
  • Check that the computer is on, awake and connected to your network. If you use Hearth Cmd on your home network only, your phone needs to be on the same network as the Keep.
  • The Keep's log is at ~/.keepcmd/daemon.log on that computer. It's designed to hold identifiers and error codes, not your content.

Household chat says it needs Away from Home

  • Household rooms travel through our relay, end-to-end encrypted. Sign this phone in from Account → Away from Home.

An agent won't start

  • Make sure the Keep is running (keepcmd status).
  • Make sure the AI software the agent uses (for example, Claude Code) is installed and signed in on the Keep's computer. Try running it directly in a terminal.
  • Check the Keep's log for errors.

An agent keeps asking for the same thing

  • Tap Always allow instead of Allow to make a rule, so matching requests go ahead without asking. Network access for agents is closed by default and builds up this way.

I'm not getting notifications

  • Notifications need this phone to be signed in (Account → Away from Home).
  • On iPhone, open Settings → Hearth Cmd → Notifications and check that notifications are allowed. If you use a Focus mode, allow Hearth Cmd in it.
  • On Android, open Settings → Apps → Hearth Cmd → Notifications and check that they're on. If you use Do Not Disturb, allow Hearth Cmd in it. Some phones also limit apps in the background to save battery: let Hearth Cmd run unrestricted.

Dictation isn't working

  • On iPhone, the first time you dictate, the app downloads its speech model; give it a moment on a good connection.
  • On Android, dictation uses your phone's speech recognition. If the app says it's preparing, the phone is downloading its on-device speech model. Check that a speech recognition service is installed and enabled in your phone's settings.

I got a new phone

  • Pair the new phone with your Keep again, or sign in with the same email address or phone number and approve it from a device you already have when the app asks.

Deleting your account

Open Account → Delete account, and confirm with the code we send you. No longer have the app? See Delete your account for how to ask by email, and for what's deleted and what's kept. Your Keep and its data stay on your computer; run keepcmd uninstall there if you want them gone. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Hearth Cmd Classic

Hearth Cmd Classic is the earlier Hearth app. It's a separate app with its own account, and its data doesn't move to Hearth Cmd. For help with it, see the Classic support page, its privacy policy and terms.
